  <div class="project-content">

    <div class="project-description">

      <p>
        Paperless working was implemented within Portsmouth Radiotherapy in
        spring 2023, using <strong>ARIA for Radiation Oncology</strong> to
        manage patients' radiotherapy notes alongside the clinical pathway,
        managed through the ARIA <strong>CarePaths</strong> module.
      </p>

      <p>
        Following a year of working with ARIA and CarePaths, a review was
        undertaken as part of continuous improvement and the Trust's strategic
        True North metric, <strong>"All of us able to improve"</strong>.
      </p>

      <p>
        The project reviews the existing CarePaths to identify opportunities
        for improvement and greater efficiency.
      </p>
