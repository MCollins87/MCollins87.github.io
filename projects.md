## CarePath Review
![CarePath Review](/images/CarePath.png)

Paperless working was implemented within Portsmouth Radiotherapy in spring 2023, using **ARIA for Radiation Oncology** to manage patients' radiotherapy notes alongside the clinical pathway, managed through the ARIA **CarePaths** module.

Following a year of working with ARIA and CarePaths, a review was undertaken as part of continuous improvement and the Trust's strategic True North metric, **"All of us able to improve"**.

The project reviews the existing CarePaths to identify opportunities for improvement and greater efficiency.
